# Seda landing page: brief

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe:** calm, exact, trustworthy, a little surprising. References: a bank statement's quiet grid; a stopwatch race replay.
2. **Journey:** see two ways of paying the same fine → watch them race → see what the bank sees → see how a bank adds a journey → see what Seda refuses to do → be invited to the live console.
3. **Energy:** calm open, the only loud moment is the race, then it settles and resolves.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment:** curiosity → amazement → clarity → control → trust → resolve. The moment: the right phone has already paid while the left one is still looking.
5. **What no site does:** the line between the two halves keeps score of every tap, and at the end it is pushed off the page.
6. **Range:** premium-minimal (user's choice). Dark, one violet accent (Seda's brand colour, no gradients), lots of air.
7. **World:** distinct scenes held in one split, not a continuous flight.
8. **Assets:** real screenshots of the Caspia app, a real Seda trace and a real AI Studio result recorded on 8 Nov 2026. No generated imagery.

## Facts used (measured, not invented)
- Shortest manual path in Caspia to pay the traffic fine: **5 taps** (Payments → fine under Due now → Pay → Confirm with fingerprint → hold sensor).
- With Seda: **1 sentence + 2 taps** (Confirm with fingerprint → hold sensor).
- Real trace: lookup_bill 600 ms, prepare 351 ms, fingerprint signs operation, execute 701 ms.
- Real AI Studio run: 4 of 4 tests passed for the traffic-fines prompt.

## Grammar
Split stage. Filmic one-shot lost because the argument has two sides; chaptered editorial lost because the proof is visual, not long-form; live surface lost because the audience is bank decision-makers, not operators (the console itself is linked at the end); continuous world, typographic poster, gallery and cutlist do not fit a comparison.

## Feeling curve
1. Curiosity: two phones, two headlines, both readable on the first screen.
2. Amazement (PEAK): the race. The right phone pays while the left is still on the Traffic fines screen; the divider's tally shows 2 against 5.
3. Clarity: the real trace slides in, every step with its time.
4. Control: one sentence becomes a tested flow in AI Studio.
5. Trust: what a voice assistant must never do, answered line by line.
6. Resolve: the divider is pushed off the left edge; Seda takes the full width with one action.
