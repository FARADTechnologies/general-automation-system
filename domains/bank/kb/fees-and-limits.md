# Fees and limits (Caspia Bank, fictional)

Transfers to Caspia cards and to saved Caspia contacts are free. Transfers to cards of other banks in Azerbaijan cost 0.5% of the amount, at least 0.30 AZN.

Transfers between your own accounts in the same currency are free and instant.

The assistant can move at most 1,000 AZN or 500 USD in one transfer, and at most 2,000 AZN or 1,000 USD per day. Larger transfers can be made at a branch or by calling the bank.

Utility and mobile bill payments are free. One bill payment is limited to 500 AZN.

International SWIFT transfers cost 0.2% of the amount, at least 15 AZN, and arrive in one to three business days. They are not available through the assistant.

Currency exchange uses the bank's rate at the moment of the transaction. There is no extra fee for exchanging between your own accounts.
