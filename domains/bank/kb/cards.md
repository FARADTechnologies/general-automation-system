# Cards

Blocking a card stops card payments and cash withdrawals immediately. Automatic payments linked to the card, such as subscriptions, also stop. You can unblock the card at any time in the app; unblocking needs fingerprint or PIN confirmation.

If your card is lost or stolen, block it first. You can then order a replacement card. A replacement card is free and is ready for pickup at the branch you choose in about three business days. The new card has a new number.

Virtual cards are for online payments only and cannot be delivered to a branch.

Daily card limits: purchases 2,000 AZN, cash withdrawals 1,000 AZN, online payments 1,500 AZN. Limits can be changed in the card settings.
