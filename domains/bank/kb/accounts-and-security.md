# Accounts, security and support

The savings account pays interest monthly. Money can be moved between the main account and the savings account at any time without a fee.

Every money movement and every security change is confirmed on the bank's own confirmation screen with your fingerprint or PIN. The assistant prepares the action; it cannot complete it without your confirmation.

The bank never asks for your PIN, CVV or one-time codes by chat, phone or e-mail. Never share them with anyone.

Recurring transfers can be scheduled monthly or weekly to a saved contact. You can stop a recurring transfer at any time in Transfers.

Payment requests send a message to a contact asking them to pay you a given amount. The contact decides whether to pay.

Support: call *0000 at any time, or ask the assistant to connect you with a bank specialist. A specialist sees what the assistant already did, so you do not have to repeat yourself.
